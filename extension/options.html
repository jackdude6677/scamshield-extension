Free public danger list (recommended)

ScamShield downloads a free public list of known phishing sites a few times a day and checks sites against it on your computer. The sites you visit are never sent anywhere. The list comes from the OpenPhish community feed.

Google Safe Browsing (optional, advanced)

ScamShield can also ask a public security database whether a site is known to be dangerous. To protect your privacy, only a short scrambled fragment of each address is sent, never the address itself. This needs a small lookup service that holds the API key, so the key is never stored in the extension. See the README for how to set one up.

Must start with https://. Leave empty to turn this off.

Your privacy

  • ScamShield has no accounts, no tracking and no ads. It never saves the sites you visit.
  • Pages are checked on your computer. Page text is never sent anywhere.
  • If you turn on the online danger list, only 8-character fragments of scrambled address codes are sent to your lookup service.
  • ScamShield cannot tell you a site is safe. It can only warn you when it sees signs of a scam.
