ScamShield

A free Chrome extension that warns you about fake bank pages, fake virus alerts and known dangerous websites. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Download ScamShield (zip)

Install in 5 steps

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it into a folder you will keep.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon and pin ScamShield.

What it does

  • Warns about addresses that copy real companies, like paypal.com.verify-account.net.
  • Warns about fake bank sign-in pages and fake "your computer is infected, call us" alerts.
  • Lets you paste a suspicious message or link to check it. Links are never opened.
  • Big, clear warnings with Go Back, Why? and an optional Continue Anyway.
Important: ScamShield can only warn you. "No warning signs found" does not mean a site is safe. If something feels wrong, stop and ask someone you trust.

Privacy

Pages are checked on your computer. Nothing you browse is saved or sent anywhere. The optional online danger list only sends short scrambled fragments of addresses, never the addresses themselves.
