ScamShield

An online guard for Chrome. It warns you about fake bank pages, fake "your computer is infected" alerts and known dangerous websites, in big, clear words.

Download ScamShield How to install

What it protects you from

Copycat websites

Addresses that imitate real companies, like paypal.com.verify-account.net.

Fake bank logins

Pages that say they are your bank and ask for your password.

Fake virus alerts

"Your computer is infected, call now" pop-ups that want money or remote access.

Suspicious messages

Paste a text or email to check it. Links are read as text and never opened.

Install in 5 steps

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it into a folder you will keep.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon and pin ScamShield.

Please remember

ScamShield can only warn you. "No warning signs found" does not mean a site is safe. If something feels wrong, stop and ask someone you trust.

Your privacy

Pages are checked on your computer. Nothing you browse is saved or sent anywhere. The optional online danger list only sends short scrambled fragments of addresses, never the addresses themselves.
